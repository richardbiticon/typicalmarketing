Creative direction

The creative is the lever.

On a performance team the creative does most of the targeting and most of the persuading. I own the direction on paid — the angle, the brief, what "on-brand and on-message" means — working with the creative team, not over them.

Direction

Sell the feeling

Towels are bought on feel and looks. Make softness and the design POV tactile — the stretch as the scroll-stopper, colour as the hook — and keep every cut unmistakably Typical.
Ownership

Brief on intent

I own the angle, the message hierarchy, and which concepts get budget. The team owns craft. Every concept ships with a tight brief: the job, the audience, the one thing it must land.
The loop

Numbers write the brief

Each test feeds back — which hook held, which angle converted, which format earned its cost — into the next brief. Winners scale; losers are documented, not repeated.

Segmentation

Who we send to
  • Lifecycle — non-buyer, one-time, repeat, lapsed, VIP / high-LTV
  • Behaviour — category & colour interest, set vs single, gifting buyers
  • Value — predicted LTV & AOV tiers for VIP and discount suppression
  • Engagement — 30/60/90-day actives; sunset dormant to protect deliverability
  • Consent — SMS-opted vs email-only, right message on the right rail
Where it meets paid
  • Suppress recent buyers from prospecting — stop paying twice
  • Seed purchaser & high-LTV as lookalike/advantage audiences
  • Hand off abandoners to retargeting + flow together
  • Build the list on every paid visit so each drop opens warm
